# Skuva

An AI-powered product catalog platform that allows ecommerce businesses, wholesalers, and retailers to import supplier product data, automatically normalize and enrich it, review AI-generated changes, and publish approved products to their ecommerce store.

## Core Features

- **Supplier Import**: Upload CSV/XLSX files.
- **AI Field Mapping**: Automatically map supplier columns to a canonical schema.
- **Catalog Normalization**: Standardize product data.
- **AI Categorization & Enrichment**: Automatically assign products to categories and generate titles, descriptions, SEO metadata, tags, and attributes.
- **Product Review**: A dedicated workspace to review, edit, and approve products.
- **Shopify Integration**: Publish approved products directly to your Shopify store.
